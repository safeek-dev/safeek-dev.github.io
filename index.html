// about_me

I started out at a product engineering shop in Coimbatore back in 2017, shipping backends for whatever landed on the desk — an online exam platform for a US university one quarter, a farm management SaaS the next, a food delivery gateway after that. Bouncing between domains taught me that systems fail in very different ways depending on what they move.

In 2023 I joined Magenta Mobility and got my first taste of talking to real hardware — thousands of EV chargers spread across cities, all chattering over OCPP. When something breaks in production it isn't a bug, it's a truck sitting cold at a charging bay. That's when I got hooked on real-time, distributed systems for a living.

Since then it's mostly been EV and energy infrastructure. Nearly two years at JioBP designing the operator control plane for one of India's largest charging networks — remote actions, Auto-Charging, OCPP over WebSockets. Now freelancing with a Swedish team on grid stabilization: same shape of problem, new domain.

If you're building distributed systems where message timing actually matters — real hardware, real money, real people waiting — I'd like to hear about it.
